Patient information

Before you visit

How do I book an appointment?
Call us on [PHONE] during OPD hours and our front desk will find a time that suits you.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Bring any X-rays, MRI or CT scans and reports you already have, even old ones. Bring a list of the medicines you take, including the dose. If another doctor has treated the same problem, bring their notes or discharge summary. Wear loose clothes so we can examine the joint easily.
When should I go to emergency instead of booking a visit?
Go to an emergency department straight away if a bone is showing through the skin, a limb looks bent or out of place, you can't move or feel your arm or leg after an injury, you've lost control of your bladder or bowel along with back pain, or a joint is hot, swollen and you have a fever.
